### Settled brief and design specification (2026-10-08)

Direction: tactile porcelain/cobalt, explicitly selected by the user through ask_user (D-05). The following token/type details are the agent's concrete design proposal within that selected direction, not separately invented user preferences.

- Six role colors: porcelain #F4F7FB, key surface #FFFFFF, ink #152238, cobalt #214ACD, secondary text/boundary #56657A, focus amber #945100. No gradients or dashboard cards. Spend the distinctive treatment on shallow raised rectangular keys and the cobalt right-hand operator rail; make Clear/Backspace secondary but legible.
- Typography: Trebuchet MS with Tahoma/Arial/sans-serif fallbacks for friendly readable controls; Consolas with Liberation Mono/monospace fallbacks and tabular numbers for the actual arithmetic/result. Use existing system fonts, no font/asset download. Title 20px, control labels 18–20px, expression 18px, result 40px mobile / 56px desktop with bounded fitting for long results.
- Desktop 1280×800: centered compact instrument, maximum approximately 720px wide; left-aligned expression/result beside a four-column keypad. Mobile 390×844 (also fit at 320px): expression/result above that same keypad; no horizontal page overflow. Do not use a landing page, hero, history panel, settings, scientific mode or a second product screen.

```text
Desktop
[ Calculator                                    ]
[ 2 + 3             ] [ Clear | Backspace        ]
[ 5                 ] [ 7 | 8 | 9 | ÷           ]
[ status / error    ] [ 4 | 5 | 6 | ×           ]
                      [ 1 | 2 | 3 | −           ]
                      [ 0 | . | = | +           ]
Mobile
[ Calculator             ]
[ 2 + 3                 5 ]
[ status / error          ]
[ Clear | Backspace       ]
[ 7 | 8 | 9 | ÷          ]
[ 4 | 5 | 6 | ×          ]
[ 1 | 2 | 3 | −          ]
[ 0 | . | = | +          ]
```

- Real copy: Calculator; Clear; Backspace; digits 0–9; decimal point; ÷ × − + =. Accessible names: Divide, Multiply, Subtract, Add, Equals. Initial display 0; arithmetic example 2 + 3 → 5; decimal example 1.5 + 2.25 → 3.75; division error “Cannot divide by zero. Clear to start again.” No marketing labels or fake stats.
- Target TARGET-CALCULATOR-WEB; required screen SCREEN-CALCULATOR; required flows FLOW-CALCULATE and FLOW-RECOVER. Required states on desktop/mobile: initial, entry, computed result, decimal result, operator-selected, divide-by-zero error, recovery after Clear, visible keyboard focus and long-result layout.
- Behavioral design assumption for this disposable prototype: basic pocket-calculator arithmetic rather than a scientific expression parser. Validate the named basic/decimal/error examples; prototype inspection is not proof of a production arithmetic engine. No extra material product choice identified.
- Accessibility: semantic named buttons, result output with appropriate polite announcement, visible two-pixel focus outline and offset, at least 44×44 CSS-pixel targets, natural tab order; digits/operators/decimal plus Enter or =, Backspace and Escape operate controls. Errors must include readable text, not color alone; selected operators have a non-color indicator. No unsolicited motion; honor prefers-reduced-motion. No hidden or clipped result/error text.
